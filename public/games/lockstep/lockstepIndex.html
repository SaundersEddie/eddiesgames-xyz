How to Play

Goal: Place one token in every row, column, and colored region.

Tokens cannot touch each other, including diagonally.

Click a square to cycle: token → X → empty.

Use X marks to track squares where a token cannot go.

Complete the board with all rules satisfied to UNLOCK the level.
